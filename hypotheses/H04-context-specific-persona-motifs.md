# H04 — Context-specific persona / voice motifs

## Claim

Some recurrent glyph or token motifs may be disproportionately associated with particular human-figure contexts and could represent remembered speech, a recurring utterance, a name, title, address, or other person-associated sound.

This is a **distributional hypothesis**, not a claim that a particular historical person has been identified.

## Predictions

For a candidate motif M:

P(M | target figure context) >> P(M | control contexts)

Possible variants M, M', M'' may form a tightly related family.

## Controls

- same manuscript section without the target figure
- same scribal hand
- labels versus running text
- token length-matched motifs

## Failure condition

If enrichment disappears after controlling for section, hand, or label/run-text differences, the persona interpretation is unsupported.
