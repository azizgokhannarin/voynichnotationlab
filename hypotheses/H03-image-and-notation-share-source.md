# H03 — Image and notation share a cognitive source

## Claim

Illustration and text may not stand in a simple caption/content relationship. They may be parallel outputs of the same remembered concept, sound, emotion, episode, or mental state.

## Model

internal state / memory
        |\
        | \
        v  v
     image notation

## Predictions

- visual oppositions or repetitions may correlate with textual oppositions/repetitions;
- pages with similar visual structure may share structural, not necessarily lexical, textual signatures;
- image changes may align with rhythm/transition changes in nearby text.

## Falsification pressure

If visual categories carry no reproducible information about textual structure after controlling for manuscript section, the hypothesis weakens substantially.
