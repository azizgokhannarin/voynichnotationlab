# H02 — Notation developed during manuscript production

## Claim

At least part of the notation system may have been invented, refined, or motorically optimized while the manuscript was being produced.

## Predictions

For glyphs or blocks absent from the earliest working sample but appearing later:

- early examples may show greater shape variance;
- later examples may become faster, more economical, or more consistent;
- new compound forms/ligatures may appear after simpler forms;
- innovation rate may decline as the system stabilizes.

## Controls

Compare candidate new glyphs with high-frequency glyphs already present at the beginning.

If all glyphs improve similarly, general handwriting drift is a better explanation.

## Major caveat

Folio order is not necessarily production order. Tests should be repeated within a consistent scribal hand and with codicological constraints where possible.
