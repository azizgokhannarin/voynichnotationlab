# H01 — Personal phonographic notation

## Hypothesis

Voynichese may encode remembered or internally generated sound through a personal visual notation rather than being a conventional substitution cipher.

This hypothesis does **not** yet assign any sound value to any glyph.

## Predictions

- Glyph/block position should show phonotactic-like constraints.
- Recurrent blocks may behave as units more consistently than arbitrary EVA character boundaries.
- Some glyphs may act as prosodic, boundary, or mode markers rather than ordinary phonemes.
- Visual contexts may correlate with recurring sound-pattern families without requiring image-to-word translation.

## Falsification pressure

Evidence against this hypothesis would include:

- no stable local transition structure beyond what a simple generative pseudo-text model explains;
- no reproducible context-sensitive motif enrichment;
- segmentation models that provide no advantage over arbitrary character groupings;
- evidence that the text is better explained by a known conventional encoding mechanism.

## Status

`SUPERSEDED_AND_BROADENED_BY_H20`

The purely phonographic framing became too narrow. H20 retains possible sound cues but permits
mixed word-shape, concept, visual, prosodic and layout reminders in a lossy private notation.
