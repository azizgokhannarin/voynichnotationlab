# H11 — Short tokens form a small number of functional classes

Many 1–3 latent-unit tokens may belong to closed or semi-closed grammatical or structural
classes rather than arbitrary lexical vocabulary.

Current surface-EVA analysis supports heterogeneous left-edge, right-edge, broad-context,
selective, and mixed classes.

A first German-style separable-particle probe on the main right-edge candidates did not find
strong narrow-stem selection or productive joined/separated surface correspondences.

## Prediction
A correct hybrid latent-unit segmentation should make the short-token functional classes
simpler and more distinct.

## Status
Functional-class hypothesis: supported structurally.
Current separable-particle candidates: weakened.
