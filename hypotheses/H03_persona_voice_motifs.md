# H03 — Recurrent persona / remembered-voice motifs

## Hypothesis

Some recurring glyph/block motifs may be disproportionately associated with recurring human-figure contexts and could encode a remembered voice, utterance pattern, prosodic signature, or other person-linked cue.

The manuscript does **not** currently justify identifying a specific historical relationship or speaker.

## Testable form

For motif M and target visual context C:

    enrichment(M,C) = P(M | C) / P(M | matched controls)

## Controls

- same scribal hand where possible;
- same section and similar text-layout type;
- matched token count;
- labels analyzed separately from running text;
- multiple-testing correction for motif scans.
