# H17 — Residual terminal classes remain after layout normalization

## Result

After excluding physical line-final tokens and shuffling terminal labels within Currier and
broad local-context strata, stem-specific terminal dependence remains far above the null.

## Interpretation

The terminal system has at least two layers:

1. layout/notational realization;
2. residual stem-associated structure.

The residual layer may be:

- grammatical morphology;
- phonotactics/phonographic encoding;
- lexical stem classes;
- a mixture.

## Required next test

Compare stem-internal phonographic predictors against external syntactic-context predictors
for terminal choice.

## Status

Residual structure supported; grammatical interpretation unresolved.
