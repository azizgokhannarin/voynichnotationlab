# H01 — Personal phonographic notation

## Claim

Voynichese may encode internally heard or remembered sound through a private visual notation system rather than a standard substitution cipher.

## Predictions

- Strong positional constraints should exist for some glyphs/blocks.
- Recurrent multi-glyph blocks may behave as units rather than arbitrary letter sequences.
- Similar visual contexts may show repeated sound-like motifs or motif families.
- Writing may become more fluent or compressed as the system matures.

## Competing explanations

- natural language in an unusual orthography
- abbreviation-heavy medieval writing
- cipher
- generated pseudo-text
- multiple scribal conventions

## Falsification pressure

The hypothesis weakens if:

- glyph/block segmentation does not produce stable contextual behavior;
- proposed sound units show no more structural coherence than arbitrary segmentations;
- apparent development is explained equally well by scribal hand differences or section differences.
