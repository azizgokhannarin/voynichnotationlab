# H06 — EVA `da` may encode a sibilant + vowel sound block

## Origin

The visual 8-like first glyph of the f78r label is EVA `d`. A published ATA mapping
assigns an S/Ş-like phonetic range to this glyph, while EVA `a` is visually compatible
with the following vowel sign in the proposed reading.

We do not adopt the ATA decipherment; we test only this local sound hypothesis.

## Corpus evidence

- EVA `d`: 10492 occurrences
- d-initial tokens: 3163
- `da...` token-initial: 1952 (61.71%)
- PMI of token-initial `da`: 2.79 bits

## Candidate readings

Test without preference:

- /sa/
- /ša/
- /se/
- /še/
- a more abstract sibilant+vowel category

## Falsification

The hypothesis weakens if a language-neutral segmentation model does not treat `da`
as a coherent unit, or if candidate-language phonotactics consistently score another
functional interpretation better.

## Status

Open — structurally motivated, phonetic value unconfirmed.
