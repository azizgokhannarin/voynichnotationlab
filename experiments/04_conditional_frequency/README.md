# Experiment 04 — Conditional frequency

For each candidate unit g measure:

- `P(g | token-start)`
- `P(g | token-end)`
- `P(g | line-start)`
- `P(g | line-end)`
- `P(g | paragraph-start)`
- `P(g | hand)`
- `P(g | section)`
- `P(g_i | g_{i-1})`

The visually q/9-like terminal sign noted during inspection will first be disambiguated against EVA/STA forms before interpreting its frequency.


## Quire 2 replication

The Quire 1 outer-to-inner D1/EVA-q rise did not reproduce in Quire 2.
Instead Quire 2 showed an approximate low-high-low-high pattern across
bifolio indices 1..4.

This shifts priority from isolated `q` frequency to the distribution of the
`qo` initial block and to quire-local state changes.
