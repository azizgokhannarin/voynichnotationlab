# OBS-0007 — User-highlighted f78r token is standalone EVA `dy`

The two-glyph word highlighted visually on f78r corresponds to RF1b-EVA locus f78r.13,
whose final token is `dy`.

This independently confirms that the visually identified 8-like + second-glyph pair is
the same `dy` construction found by corpus analysis.

Standalone `dy` is common enough for quantitative analysis (205 instances) and is enriched
at line endings, but most instances remain line-medial.
