# OBS-0005 — Quire 1 outer bifolio is unusually `qo`-poor

## Observation

Full RF1b-EVA scan:

- Q1/B1: 5/558 = 0.90% token-initial `qo`
- Q1/B4: 25/239 = 10.46%
- effect ratio B4/B1: 11.67x

The three herbal A/hand-1 pages on Q1/B1 are among the lowest-ranked comparable pages
for `qo` frequency. f1r is a different layout class (`I=T`) and separately has zero q.

## Interpretation limit

This is not evidence that `qo` was invented on Q1. Content, text state, planning order,
or a structural-prefix explanation remain viable.
