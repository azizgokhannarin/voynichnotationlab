# OBS-0010 — Short tokens share inferred predicate/morphological stem families

Using only surface EVA prefixes and final 1–2 glyph variation, several frequent short
tokens are followed by overlapping stem families substantially more often than random
pair similarity would suggest.

Some short-token pairs also show different preferred endings within individual shared
stem families.

This is compatible with grammatical agreement, but the effect is not yet a repeated,
clean multi-person paradigm. It may also arise from other function-word or morphological
structure.
