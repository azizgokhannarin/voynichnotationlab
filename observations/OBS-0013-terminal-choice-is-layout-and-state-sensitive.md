# OBS-0013 — Terminal choice is layout- and state-sensitive within the same stems

In five-fold whole-page held-out prediction, adding line position or Currier state improves
terminal prediction beyond stem identity alone.

Most strikingly, EVA `m` has a within-stem Mantel-Haenszel line-final odds ratio of
16.9 and a stratified line-final
z-score of 35.8.

In productive stems that also have `r/l` alternatives, `m` is line-final about
66.2% of the time, versus 8.0% for
the corresponding `r/l` forms.

This requires the terminal model to distinguish linguistic terminal structure from a
layout/notational layer.
