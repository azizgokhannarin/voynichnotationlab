# OBS-0018 — No transferable EVA paragraph-stop terminal on TRAIN

## Observation

When paragraph-final words are compared only with other physical-line-final words, terminal
identity adds no robust transferable paragraph-closure signal after conditioning on the preceding
raw glyph or frozen stem-final proxy. Leave-one-page-out AUC is 0.52–0.57 across ZL3b and IT2a,
and conditional information does not exceed the within-page permutation 99th percentile.

An injected deterministic allomorph using the same terminal inventory is recovered at AUC
0.999, confirming instrument power.

## Interpretation

No single EVA terminal and no small preceding-ending-to-EVA-terminal mapping can currently be
identified as a mandatory paragraph point. A micrographic allograph collapsed by transcription
remains open and becomes the sole next target.

## Limits

Paragraph ends are transcriber annotations pending blind image adjudication. This result does not
exclude untranscribed flourishes, spacing-only closure, or a closure operation outside the tested
one-terminal representation.
