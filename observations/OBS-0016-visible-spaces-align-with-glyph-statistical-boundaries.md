# OBS-0016 — Visible spaces align with glyph-statistical boundaries

Five-fold whole-page held-out analysis, with spaces removed from the training streams,
produces above-chance discrimination of actual manuscript spaces from internal glyph cuts.

Best AUC: 0.833.
Mean page AUC for that metric:
0.836.

This supports retaining visible-space tokens as the working segmentation layer.
