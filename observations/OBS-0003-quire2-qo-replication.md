# OBS-0003 — Quire 2 falsifies a universal outer-to-inner q gradient

## Observation

Quire 2 EVA-q rates per 100 tokens by physical bifolio:

- B1: 7.10
- B2: 11.71
- B3: 6.94
- B4: 11.76 (incomplete surviving layer)

This is not monotonic.

## Strong secondary observation

89 of 90 Quire 2 q occurrences are token-initial, and all 89 token-initial q occurrences are immediately followed by o.

## Interpretation

The Quire 1 D1 gradient is not a generic property of nested bifolio depth.

The relevant object for future phonographic segmentation is more plausibly `qo` than isolated `q`.

Replicate on additional quires before interpreting the odd/even alternation.
