# OBS-0002 — D1 / EVA-q rises across Quire 1 bifolio layers

## Observation

In RF1b reduced STA, D1 frequency rises monotonically across Quire 1's nested physical bifolios:

- B=1 (f1+f8): 0.249%
- B=2 (f2+f7): 1.837%
- B=3 (f3+f6): 2.479%
- B=4 (f4+f5): 2.829%

The inner/outer ratio is ~11.37x.

## What this does not prove

- It does not prove writing order.
- It does not prove D1 represents a newly invented sound.
- It does not prove a single author.
- It does not distinguish phonetic change from grammatical/content change.

## Why it matters

The gradient follows physical bifolio layer, so it cannot be explained merely as a f1->f8 reading-order artifact.
