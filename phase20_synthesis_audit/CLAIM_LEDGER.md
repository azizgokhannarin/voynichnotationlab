# Cross-phase claim ledger after Phase 20

Date: 2026-08-26

## Approved claims

1. Direct phonographic substitution at the frozen exact-unit,
   fixed-word-boundary and bounded-homophony level is unsupported across the
   four tested languages.
2. Voynich exact-token choices are strongly allocated to particular pages and
   physical lines, while their order inside a fixed line inventory is not
   significantly different from the frozen shuffle null.
3. Surface line-locality, class-lag decay and distant-context nulls cannot
   distinguish hidden natural-language content behind a strong renderer from
   autonomous generation.
4. Ordinary diplomatic abbreviated Latin can exhibit a line-local predictive
   advantage and negligible cross-line gain.
5. A real unordered transaction/tally control can reproduce the qualitative
   allocation-without-order recurrence phenotype, although at substantially
   different effect sizes.
6. The certified residual-capacity upper bound does not reject content-rich
   hidden content; its magnitude is not evidence that content is present.
7. The frozen binary line-state model does not show robust transferable gain
   after page bootstrap and prequential complexity control.
8. A simple first-order line-reset generator is insufficient; the bounded
   copy/modify, line-final and innovation generator remains untested.
9. H_C, H_D and H_G remain open and unranked. H_T remains an open confound.

## Prohibited claims

- “Voynich is not language.”
- “Voynich is procedural pseudo-text.”
- “Voynich is structured records or numerical notation.”
- “Line reset or line-final `m` proves autonomous generation.”
- “The structured-data phenotype match proves the same mechanism.”
- “High residual capacity proves hidden content.”
- “No latent state exists.”
- “The high fitted HMM transition persistence is a real manuscript state.”
- “Current evidence favors H_C, H_D or H_G.”
- “The prior internal-evidence STOP condition has been reached.”

## Scope language

Use “unsupported at the frozen representation/model level” for closures and
“not rejected” for surviving broad hypotheses. Use “qualitative phenotype
match” rather than “mechanism match” for Phase 17. Use “upper bound” whenever
reporting Phase-18 capacity. Use “no robust binary line-state signal under the
frozen model” for Phase 19.
